# Customer Context

## Overview

Customers use the MATTHEWS CORP mobile app to manage their financial products and expect timely, relevant, and trustworthy communication.

The app is typically accessed frequently for tasks such as checking balances, reviewing transactions, and completing key actions.

Push notifications play an important role in keeping customers informed and engaged outside of active app usage.

---

## Customer Behaviour

- Customers check the app regularly, often in response to specific triggers (e.g. transactions, payments, alerts)
- Customers rely on notifications for time-sensitive updates and actions
- Customers are sensitive to the volume and relevance of notifications
- Customers may disengage or opt out if communications feel excessive or unnecessary
- Customers often make quick decisions based on notifications (e.g. opening the app, taking action, or ignoring)

---

## Customer Needs

Customers expect:

- Clear and relevant notifications
- Timely delivery of important information
- Confidence that critical alerts will reach them
- Simple and intuitive control over what they receive
- Transparency about why they are receiving notifications

---

## Pain Points

Common customer frustrations include:

- Receiving too many notifications
- Receiving notifications that are not relevant
- Lack of control over notification types
- Uncertainty about what notifications they are opted into
- Difficulty adjusting preferences once notifications become overwhelming

---

## Expectations of Push Notifications

Customers expect push notifications to be:

- Relevant to their activity and needs
- Easy to understand at a glance
- Actionable where appropriate
- Used sparingly and appropriately
- Trustworthy, particularly for security-related messages

---

## Trust and Perception

- Customers associate push notifications with both convenience and risk
- Overuse of notifications can reduce trust and lead to disengagement
- Poorly targeted or irrelevant messages can be perceived as spam
- Clear and predictable behaviour builds confidence in the app

---

## Segmentation (High-Level)

### Highly Engaged Users
- Frequently use the app
- More tolerant of relevant notifications
- Likely to engage with timely updates

### Passive Users
- Use the app less frequently
- More sensitive to notification volume
- More likely to opt out if overwhelmed

### Notification-Resistant Users
- Have disabled or rarely engage with notifications
- Require clear value to re-engage

---

## Behavioural Insights

- Customers are more likely to engage with:
  - time-sensitive alerts
  - account-related notifications
- Customers are less likely to engage with:
  - generic promotional messages
  - repeated or similar notifications
- Customers often prefer fewer, more relevant notifications over higher volume

---

## Implications for Product Decisions

- Notification relevance is more important than volume
- Giving customers control can reduce full opt-outs
- Clear categorisation helps customers make better decisions
- Over-communication increases disengagement risk
- Critical notifications must remain prominent and reliable
