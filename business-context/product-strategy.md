# Product Strategy

## Overview

MATTHEWS CORP is a mobile-first financial services platform offering a range of consumer financial products.

The mobile app is the primary customer interaction channel, used for account management, transactions, and ongoing engagement.

Push notifications are a key channel for real-time communication, customer engagement, and product promotion.

---

## Strategic Objectives

- Increase active engagement within the mobile app
- Improve customer retention and reduce churn
- Drive adoption of additional financial products
- Increase effectiveness of digital communication channels
- Strengthen customer trust and perceived value

---

## Role of Push Notifications

Push notifications are a core part of the engagement strategy and are used to:

- Deliver timely and relevant account updates
- Prompt user actions (e.g. payments, approvals)
- Support security and verification flows
- Drive awareness of product features and offers

Push notifications are expected to be:
- timely
- relevant
- actionable
- trusted

---

## Key Challenges

- Low opt-in rates reduce overall reach
- Overuse or irrelevant messaging leads to disengagement
- Customers lack control and visibility over notifications
- Marketing communications have significantly lower engagement than service notifications

---

## Strategic Principles

### 1. Deliver Value Over Volume
Notifications should provide clear value to the customer. Avoid sending messages that do not drive meaningful action or awareness.

### 2. Prioritise Relevance
Notifications should be tailored to customer context, behaviour, and needs wherever possible.

### 3. Maintain Trust
Customers must trust that notifications are useful and not excessive. Over-communication erodes trust and increases opt-outs.

### 4. Protect Critical Communications
Security and account-related notifications are essential and must remain reliable and effective.

### 5. Enable Customer Control
Customers should have clear, simple ways to control what they receive, without compromising critical communication.

---

## Success Definition

Push notifications are successful when:

- Customers remain opted in
- Notifications drive meaningful engagement or action
- Customers do not feel overwhelmed or disrupted
- Critical communications are reliably delivered and acted upon

---

## Implications for Product Decisions

- Avoid designs that maximise notification volume without considering relevance
- Separate critical and non-critical communications clearly
- Provide transparency around what notifications are sent and why
- Ensure preference controls are intuitive and meaningful
- Balance business goals (engagement, growth) with customer experience and trust
