# Push Notification Metrics

## Overview

Push notifications are a key engagement and communication channel within the MATTHEWS CORP mobile app.

Current performance indicates low adoption and uneven engagement, suggesting issues with relevance, volume, and customer control.

---

## Adoption

- Push notification opt-in rate: **48%**
- Push notification opt-out rate: **52%**
- Percentage of users disabling notifications within 7 days of install: **18%**

---

## Engagement

- Average notification open rate: **22%**
- Engagement rate for account-related notifications: **35%**
- Engagement rate for security notifications: **42%**
- Engagement rate for marketing notifications: **12%**

---

## Notification Volume

- Percentage of users receiving >5 notifications per week: **40%**
- Average notifications per active user per week: **4.8**
- High-frequency segments (>8 notifications/week): **15% of users**

---

## Customer Behaviour Signals

- Increase in notification opt-outs following periods of high notification volume
- Lower engagement observed for repeated or similar notification types
- Higher engagement for time-sensitive or action-driven notifications

---

## Customer Feedback

Common themes from customer feedback:

- “Too many notifications”
- “Notifications aren’t relevant”
- “I don’t know what I’m opting into”
- “No easy way to control what I receive”

---

## Risk & Critical Communications

- Security/fraud notification delivery success rate: **>95%**
- High reliance on push notifications for time-sensitive actions (e.g. approvals)
- Low tolerance for missed critical notifications

---

## Key Insights

- Low opt-in rate suggests lack of perceived value or trust
- High notification volume likely contributes to notification fatigue
- Marketing notifications have significantly lower engagement than service-related notifications
- Customers are more likely to disable all notifications rather than manage preferences
- Critical notifications perform well but rely on overall push enablement

---

## Success Metrics (Target State)

- Increase push notification opt-in rate to **>60%**
- Increase overall notification engagement rate
- Improve engagement with marketing notifications
- Reduce OS-level notification opt-outs
- Increase usage of notification preferences settings
- Maintain or improve effectiveness of critical notifications
