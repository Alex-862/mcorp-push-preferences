# Initiative: Improve Notification Preference Transparency

## Problem

Customers have limited understanding of:
- what notifications they are receiving
- why they are receiving them
- the impact of disabling categories

This contributes to low opt-in rates and disengagement.

---

## Scope

This initiative will focus on:
- improving category descriptions
- introducing examples of notifications
- explaining the impact of disabling categories

This initiative will NOT include:
- granular notification controls
- notification frequency controls
- redesign of notification delivery systems
- changes to notification categories

---

## Success Metrics

- Increase engagement with notification preferences screen
- Reduce notification opt-outs
- Improve customer understanding of notification categories

---

## Constraints

- Existing category model remains unchanged
- Existing backend notification tagging remains unchanged
- No changes to OS-level permissions
