# What changed in the updated prototype

The core journey is the same — customers still navigate to More, then Notifications, and use toggles to manage their preferences. The three categories (Account Activity, Security & Verification, Marketing & Promotions) are unchanged.

What's different is how much information the customer has when making those decisions.

**Clearer descriptions**
Each category now explains what it actually includes, rather than just showing a label. For example, Account Activity tells you it covers transaction updates, payment confirmations, and account status changes — so you know exactly what you're turning on or off.

**Example notifications**
Every category shows two real examples of the kinds of messages you'd receive. This gives customers a concrete sense of what they're opting into, rather than having to guess.

**A pause before turning something off**
In the original prototype, tapping a toggle turned it off immediately with no further context. In the updated version, tapping to disable a category shows a short message explaining what the customer will stop receiving, and asks them to confirm or cancel. Nothing is saved until they make that choice. Turning something back on still happens immediately — the pause only applies when disabling.

**A stronger warning for Security & Verification**
If a customer tries to turn off the Security & Verification category, they see an additional amber warning on top of the standard message. This makes clear that disabling this category could mean missing fraud alerts or authentication requests. The customer can still proceed — they're not blocked — but they can't do it without being clearly informed of the risk.
